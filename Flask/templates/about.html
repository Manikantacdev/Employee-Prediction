Enter employee and work-related parameters including department, team size, targeted productivity, overtime hours, incentives, and other relevant metrics.

Step 2: AI Analysis

Our machine learning model processes the data, analyzing patterns and relationships between various factors that influence productivity.

Step 3: Generate Insights

The system generates comprehensive visualizations including correlation heatmaps, distribution charts, and comparative analytics.

Step 4: Get Results

Receive detailed productivity predictions categorized as average, medium, or high, along with actionable insights for improvement.
